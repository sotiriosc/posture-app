## Teaching Core

### focus

- `bird-dog.focus.contralateral-reach`: Reach one arm and the opposite leg while the trunk remains organized over the floor contacts.

### cues

- `bird-dog.cue.push-floor`: Press the supporting hand and knee into the floor.
- `bird-dog.cue.reach-long`: Reach long through the moving hand and heel instead of chasing height.
- `bird-dog.cue.quiet-pelvis`: Keep the pelvis facing the floor as the limbs move.

### setup

- `bird-dog.setup.quadruped`: Begin on hands and knees with clear floor space and stable support contacts.
- `bird-dog.setup.stack`: Place hands under shoulders and knees under hips before selecting the first side.
- `bird-dog.equipment.floor`: This task requires bodyweight floor space; no external load or unstable surface is implied.

### during

- `bird-dog.during.opposite-limbs`: Move the selected arm and opposite leg together only as far as trunk position remains controlled.
- `bird-dog.during.return`: Return both moving limbs to quadruped control before the next repetition or side.
- `bird-dog.during.side-explicit`: Follow the prescribed side or alternating sequence explicitly.

### pattern

- `bird-dog.pattern.contralateral-trunk`: Bird Dog is a quadruped contralateral trunk-control task, not a plank or a default back-pain exercise.

- `bird-dog.watch.lumbar-extension`: Watch for reaching the leg upward by changing the trunk position.
- `bird-dog.watch.rushed-switch`: Watch for switching sides before stable quadruped contact is restored.
