# Session Composition Evaluation Policy

Hard-invalid branches are removed first. Complete skeletons compare strictly in this order:

1. complete required coverage and valid composition;
2. no urgent or role-substitution state;
3. lower candidate-review burden;
4. lower unjustified productive-anchor displacement;
5. complete required dependency coherence;
6. dominant required main purpose;
7. lower non-anchor prescription-resolution burden;
8. preferred coverage in Planner `priorityOrder`;
9. lower redundancy/conflict;
10. lower fatigue/stress potential concentration;
11. optional coverage in Planner `priorityOrder`;
12. fewer identities;
13. fewer structural setup transitions;
14. local Candidate Intelligence rank vector;
15. canonical identity tie-break.

Candidate totals are never summed. Raw anchor/preferred/optional counts are never rewarded. Preferred/optional standalone work is admitted only by structural-capacity and burden policy, then compared with/without the identity. `NO_POSITIVE_MARGINAL_VALUE` excludes filler.

Continuity measures unjustified displacement, not anchor count. Changed need, illegality, equipment loss, explicit block, unresolved review, non-resolvable pain, adverse response, failed progression, or a higher-order coherence reason can justify non-retention.
