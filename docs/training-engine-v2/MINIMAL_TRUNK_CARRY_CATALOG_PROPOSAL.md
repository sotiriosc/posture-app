## Current Gap Statement

The current catalog directly represents breathing/position, early anti-extension through Dead Bug, and anti-rotation through Pallof Press. It lacks a truthful direct candidate pool for anti-lateral flexion, controlled trunk flexion, controlled trunk rotation, later anti-extension, intentional loaded bracing, bilateral and unilateral carries, a supported stationary carry regression, and capacity-oriented loaded gait.

## Minimal Tranche Summary

Candidates considered: 24. Selected: 7. Deferred: 15. Rejected as standalone current-V2 IDs: 2.

### Selected for First Implementation

- forearm-plank: Forearm Plank
- forearm-side-plank: Forearm Side Plank
- suitcase-carry: Suitcase Carry
- machine-abdominal-crunch: Machine Abdominal Crunch
- half-kneeling-high-to-low-cable-chop: Half-Kneeling High-to-Low Cable Chop
- farmer-carry: Bilateral Farmer Carry
- wall-supported-suitcase-march: Wall-Supported Suitcase March

### Deferred for Later

- stability-ball-rollout: Defer until stability-ball capability, range, and shoulder/kneeling stress contracts are reviewed.
- ab-wheel-rollout: Defer until ab-wheel capability and loaded shoulder/wrist/kneeling stress mappings are accepted.
- barbell-rollout: Defer because ordinary barbell availability does not prove a compatible rolling setup.
- suitcase-hold: Defer as a separate row until static hold versus march/carry identity and prescription units are settled.
- kneeling-cable-crunch: Defer because machine flexion is the cleaner first bootstrap and cable anchor/pelvis/kneeling contracts remain open.
- reverse-crunch: Defer until controlled trunk flexion can be separated from hip-dominant motion and a bodyweight prescription contract exists.
- hanging-knee-raise: Defer because grip/shoulder prerequisites and hip-versus-trunk function ambiguity make it a poor first flexion bootstrap.
- standing-cable-chop: Defer because standing hip/pelvis/stance variability is less clean than the first half-kneeling definition.
- band-chop: Defer until the cable definition is reviewed and band tension/path can add real equipment diversity without ambiguity.
- cable-lift: Defer directional expansion until one controlled-rotation definition and stress contract are accepted.
- front-rack-carry: Defer because Farmer Carry supplies bilateral coverage with fewer rack-position prerequisites and less first-tranche redundancy.
- front-rack-march: Defer because the selected wall-supported suitcase march supplies stationary regression coverage with less rack complexity.
- front-rack-hold: Defer because Farmer and Suitcase Carry already cover intentional loaded bracing in the minimum and rack demands add complexity.
- goblet-brace-hold: Defer because selected carries already cover loaded bracing and this hold would add overlapping exposure before hold prescription is structured.
- overhead-carry: Defer from the first tranche because shoulder/scapular prerequisites, overhead stress, clearance, and loaded-gait contracts are materially broader.

### Rejected for Current V2

- long-lever-plank: Reject as a separate catalog row; represent it as reviewed Forearm Plank lever progression to avoid a near-duplicate.
- bent-knee-side-plank: Reject as a separate catalog row; preserve it as the support/lever regression of Side Plank.

Rejection here means no separate first-tranche reference-exercise identity. Long-Lever Plank and Bent-Knee Side Plank remain possible same-exercise lever/support progressions; no movement concept is rejected from human practice by this catalog decision.

## Role and Mechanics Distinctions

- MovementRole is reviewed selection-purpose truth; a mechanics level cannot grant a role.
- TrunkMechanicsProfile records reviewed function expression during execution; it does not make the exercise directly selectable for every expressed function.
- Side Plank proposes anti_lateral_flexion_core without carry, loaded_bracing, or gait selection roles.
- Farmer Carry proposes carry and loaded_bracing roles; bilateral loading may express anti-lateral control without granting an anti_lateral_flexion_core role.
- Suitcase Carry proposes carry, anti_lateral_flexion_core, and loaded_bracing only as explicit owner-review items, not as inferred consequences of its mechanics.
- Half-Kneeling High-to-Low Cable Chop proposes trunk_rotation and controlled rotation; Pallof Press remains anti_rotation_core with no controlled-rotation equivalence.
