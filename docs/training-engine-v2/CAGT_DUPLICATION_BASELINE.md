# CAGT Duplication Baseline

CAGT reports framework, weekly objective, reservation, anchor, supporting-work, exact-session, optional-accessory, assessment, P0, exercise identity, Prescription-handoff, and rep recurrence separately.

Recurrence is classified as `PRODUCTIVE_STABILITY`, `EXPECTED_FRAMEWORK`, `JUSTIFIED_SHARED_SOLUTION`, `OPTIONAL_REDUNDANCY`, `ASSESSMENT_OVERREPETITION`, `TEMPLATE_COLLISION`, or `UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_REVIEW`.

V1 measures framework recurrence `0.7` and weekly adaptive responsibility recurrence from the ten-user cohort. Session identity, P0, Prescription, and rep recurrence that cannot yet be truthfully observed remain null. No aggregate duplication number or failure threshold is created.
