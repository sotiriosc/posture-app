# CAGT Duplication Baseline

CAGT reports framework, weekly objective, reservation, anchor, supporting-work, exact-session, optional-accessory, assessment, P0, exercise identity, Prescription-handoff, and rep recurrence separately.

Recurrence is classified as `PRODUCTIVE_STABILITY`, `EXPECTED_FRAMEWORK`, `JUSTIFIED_SHARED_SOLUTION`, `OPTIONAL_REDUNDANCY`, `ASSESSMENT_OVERREPETITION`, `TEMPLATE_COLLISION`, or `UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_REVIEW`.

V1 measures framework recurrence `0.7` and weekly adaptive responsibility recurrence from the ten-user cohort. Session identity, P0, Prescription, and rep recurrence that cannot yet be truthfully observed remain null. No aggregate duplication number or failure threshold is created.

The numeric tournament adds per-candidate reservation recurrence, objectives per reservation, direct recurrence, assessment recurrence, optional recurrence, and zero-marginal-value counts. These remain separate semantic observations; no weighted duplication score exists. A2 recurrence, optional zero-value work, and condensed-session overload are executable Gate 4 fail-stop probes.

## Preparation Recurrence

The coherent-session extension keeps warm-up and activation recurrence separate from weekly dose and assessment recurrence. Required dependency recurrence and productive continuity may pass; generic filler, optional redundancy, stale recurrence, cross-section identity duplication, assessment-cluster multiplication, and preparation counted as weekly dose fail independently. No hard count or diversity target is introduced.
