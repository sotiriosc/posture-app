## Binding Decisions

1. An allocation opportunity is responsibility to provide a truthful opportunity, not dose, completion, or adaptation credit. Allocation, planned Prescription/source exposure, and completed performance/response remain separate ledgers.
2. Objective frequency requires explicit reviewed authority. Missing authority returns `FREQUENCY_POLICY_REQUIRED`; equally authoritative conflicts return `WEEKLY_POLICY_CONFLICT`. Goal, phase, experience, target name, profile, or opportunity count creates no default.
3. `minimumAllocatedSessions` is a visible floor, `targetAllocatedSessions` is a preferred distribution target, and `softMaximumAllocatedSessions` is a review state. None grants dose credit; soft maximum is not a hard ceiling.
4. Direct objectives require `primary_required` or exact required action/function truth. A general development objective may use `primary_preferred` or `any_meaningful_contributor` only when explicitly selected. No fractional set-equivalence coefficient is approved.
5. One coherent assessment cluster creates at most one weekly assessment objective. Repetition above one needs policy or coach authority; per-session preparation remains a Planner decision.
6. Recovery spacing has typed scope, provenance, strength, basis, and Prescription dependency. Opportunity order is not elapsed time, and no universal hours or nonconsecutive-day rule is approved.
7. Phase is policy applicability, not a multiplier. It creates no frequency, volume, split, recovery interval, accessory count, or automatic rebuild.
8. Deload is longitudinally owned. No calendar-only deload is approved. Standalone recovery sessions remain `KEEP_DEFERRED`.
9. Week search may use an exact oracle, deterministic Pareto/dominance pruning, strict lexicographic complete-plan comparison, and explicit truncation. Bounds remain unapproved; weighted totals, fixed splits, greedy first-fit, repair loops, and fallback weeks are prohibited.
10. Each objective records structured goal relationships. Each reservation records weekly primary and secondary goals plus one session-specific goal justified by dominant objective evidence. The directive receives the session goal.
11. An ordinary session has exactly one required dominant responsibility: `dominant_main` or `capacity_main`. `capacity_main` maps to main/capacity and cannot coexist with another dominant responsibility.
12. Loaded carry/bracing work capacity is supported; local muscular capacity is partial; systemic conditioning needs a Prescription contract; unrepresented interval/cardio needs catalog or modality support. Carries do not stand in for all conditioning.
13. Product Adapter facts are not programming policy. Calendar free time and profile defaults are tentative until appropriately confirmed. Horizon revisions are immutable and day-of actual facts are supplied again.

No numeric frequency, set, volume, spacing, deload, or search-bound value is activated by this record.
