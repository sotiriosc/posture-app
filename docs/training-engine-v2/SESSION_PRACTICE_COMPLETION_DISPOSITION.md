# Session Practice Completion Disposition

`SESSION_PRACTICE_COMPLETION_DISPOSITION@1.0.0` derives completion only from actual realized evidence.
Full may be `full_completed_as_prescribed`; Lighter may be complete with all required responsibility or
partial with open responsibility; Recovery is
`recovery_support_completed_original_responsibility_unfulfilled`. Partial, not-completed, abandoned,
incomplete-evidence, and conflict states remain explicit. Every result applies zero automatic
reallocation, doubling, or adaptation action.
