## Teaching Core

### focus

- `side-lying-hip-abduction.focus.lateral-lift`: Lift the top leg from the hip while the pelvis remains stacked.

### cues

- `side-lying-hip-abduction.cue.long-leg`: Reach the top leg long as it lifts and lowers.
- `side-lying-hip-abduction.cue.toes-forward`: Keep the top leg oriented consistently instead of turning the toes upward to gain range.
- `side-lying-hip-abduction.cue.quiet-waist`: Keep the side of the waist quiet against the floor support.

### setup

- `side-lying-hip-abduction.setup.side-lying`: Lie on one side with the head and torso supported and the working leg on top.
- `side-lying-hip-abduction.setup.lower-leg`: Bend or position the lower leg to create a stable base while keeping the pelvis stacked.
- `side-lying-hip-abduction.equipment.floor-bodyweight`: The admitted realization requires floor space and uses bodyweight; a band is not part of this realization.

### during

- `side-lying-hip-abduction.during.abduct`: Move the top leg away from the midline without rolling the pelvis backward.
- `side-lying-hip-abduction.during.control-return`: Lower the leg with control until the next repetition can begin from the same side-lying position.
- `side-lying-hip-abduction.during.side-owned`: Complete the prescribed side explicitly rather than assuming bilateral credit from one set.

### pattern

- `side-lying-hip-abduction.pattern.floor-abduction`: A side-lying bodyweight hip-abduction task trains the top leg against gravity from substantial floor support.

### watchFor

- `side-lying-hip-abduction.watch.pelvis-roll`: Watch for the pelvis rolling backward as the leg lifts.
- `side-lying-hip-abduction.watch.trunk-sidebend`: Watch for side-bending the trunk to create extra leg height.
