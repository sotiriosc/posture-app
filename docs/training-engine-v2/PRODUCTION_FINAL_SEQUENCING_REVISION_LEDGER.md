# Production Final Sequencing Revision Ledger

Generated deterministically from the inactive production Final Session Sequencing kernel.

The ledger is append-only, explicitly timed, and scoped to one plan and execution attempt. Revisions retain ancestry, reason, changed refs, policy, Prescription revisions, transition facts, search policy, and provenance; supersession is a separate immutable record.

- Stress revision chains: `1000`
- Chain failures: `0`
- Completed revisions rewritten: `0`

Persistence is not wired.
