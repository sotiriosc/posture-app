## Teaching Core

### focus

- `dumbbell-floor-press.focus.floor-bounded-press`: Press the dumbbells upward while the upper arms finish each lowering phase on the floor.

### cues

- `dumbbell-floor-press.cue.wrist-over-elbow`: Keep each wrist stacked over its elbow through the press.
- `dumbbell-floor-press.cue.steady-base`: Keep the feet, pelvis, and upper back steady as the dumbbells move.
- `dumbbell-floor-press.cue.controlled-touch`: Lower with control until the upper arms make a quiet floor contact.

### setup

- `dumbbell-floor-press.setup.supine-floor`: Lie supine with the upper back and pelvis supported by clear floor space.
- `dumbbell-floor-press.setup.feet-supported`: Place both feet securely and begin with the dumbbells controlled beside the torso.
- `dumbbell-floor-press.equipment.no-bench`: This realization requires floor space and one or two dumbbells; a bench is not required.

### during

- `dumbbell-floor-press.during.vertical-forearms`: Maintain a forearm position that remains close to vertical while pressing and lowering.
- `dumbbell-floor-press.during.bounded-range`: Use the floor contact as the bottom boundary instead of forcing additional shoulder range.
- `dumbbell-floor-press.during.finish-controlled`: Finish the set only after the dumbbells are controlled back to a stable start position.

### pattern

- `dumbbell-floor-press.pattern.horizontal-press`: A floor-supported horizontal press uses floor contact to bound the lowering range.

### watchFor

- `dumbbell-floor-press.watch.elbows-drifting`: Watch for elbows drifting far from the forearm line.
- `dumbbell-floor-press.watch.bouncing`: Watch for bouncing the upper arms off the floor to start the press.
- `dumbbell-floor-press.watch.unstable-finish`: Watch for a rushed finish that leaves a dumbbell uncontrolled.
