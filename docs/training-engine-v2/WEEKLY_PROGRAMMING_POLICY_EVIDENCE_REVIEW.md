## Interpretation Boundary

Overall participation, objective allocation frequency, prescribed muscle dose, direct versus secondary contribution, completed exposure, and individual response are different facts. Frequency findings cannot become dose; volume findings cannot become allocation frequency. Recovery evidence does not support universal hours independent of dose, target, population, and response. Clinical guidance applies only to its reviewed population and cannot silently control the general engine.

Effect estimates and certainty differ by outcome, training status, model, and included studies. The evidence inventory therefore supports owner review of candidates, not automatic values. Exact Prescription dose ownership and Longitudinal response ownership remain prerequisites for prescribed weekly validation.
