# Prescription Tempo Duration Evidence Review

Evidence boundary: current evidence supports policy review, not universal numeric defaults.

| Source | Population / mode | Applicability | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ACSM 2026 Position Stand, PMID 41843416, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843416/ | Healthy adults; overview of reviews across resistance-training prescription variables | `SUPPORTED_FOR_POLICY_REVIEW`; broad resistance-training prescription context | No universal tempo, pause, hold duration, breathing cadence, or carry pace for this engine |
| Schoenfeld et al. 2015, PMID 25601394, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25601394/ | Resistance training repetition duration and hypertrophy | `BROAD_FLEXIBILITY_PRIOR`; repetition duration can vary without proving one universal tempo | No universal 3-1-1 or slow-tempo hypertrophy rule |
| Amdi et al. 2025, PMID 40692176, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40692176/ | Eccentric phase duration meta-analysis | `GOAL_SPECIFIC`; eccentric duration may affect some outcomes under reviewed conditions | No automatic longer-eccentric progression or hypertrophy score |
| Enes et al. 2025, DOI 10.1519/JSC.0000000000005302, https://www.ovid.com/jnls/nsca-jscr/abstract/10.1519/jsc.0000000000005302~how-slow-should-you-go-a-systematic-review-with | Resistance-training repetition tempo and hypertrophy | `BROAD_FLEXIBILITY_PRIOR`; tempo effects appear small/conditional in available summaries | No universal numeric tempo policy |

Breathing cadence, isometric duration, carry pace, march cadence, older/adapted-population dosing, and exercise-specific timing remain `EXTERNAL_REFERENCE_PENDING` or `PRESCRIPTION_DEPENDENT` unless future reviewed policy supplies authority.

Policy exclusions:

- no universal three-second eccentric;
- no universal pause;
- no universal explosive concentric;
- no universal 30-second hold;
- no time-under-tension hypertrophy score;
- no set-equivalence coefficient;
- no medical safety claim.
