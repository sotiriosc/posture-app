# Centration cues — review for Sotirios

Warmup and activation drills now carry structured joint-centration coaching
(`Set up` / `During` / `Pattern` / `Watch for`) in
`packages/engine/src/centrationCues.ts`.

Please scan below **before treating this as final coaching voice**. Grouped by
movement pattern. Order inside each group follows beginner-program frequency
(most-used first).

Source principles used (not invented):

- Ribcage-over-pelvis stacking / canister / zone of apposition
- Neutral pelvis for force transfer
- Scapulae sitting and gliding on the ribcage (protraction, retraction, upward
  rotation as relevant)
- Joint-specific alignment for the drill’s target

---

## Spine mobility

### `cat-cow` — Cat-Cow Flow
- **Set up:** Hands/knees stacked; mid-position ribs/pelvis; long neck
- **During:** Cow/cat from mid-back; pelvis–ribs as one sequence
- **Pattern:** Coordinated spine motion with ribs/pelvis related
- **Watch for:** End-range lumbar hang; shrugging instead of T-spine motion

### `thoracic-rotation` — Thoracic Rotation
- **Set up:** Hips stacked/quiet; exhale to settle ribs
- **During:** Rotate from upper spine; stop when pelvis wants to roll
- **Pattern:** T-spine rotation without lumbar borrow
- **Watch for:** Pelvis rolling open; neck/low-back forcing

---

## Scapular / T-spine (wall + prone + band)

### `wall-slides` — Wall Slides
- **Set up:** Wall contacts + ribs stacked (no lumbar cheat); goal-post arms
- **During:** Scapulae glide on ribs; shorten range before ribs flare
- **Pattern:** Overhead scapular glide with stacked canister
- **Watch for:** Rib flare for wall contact; shrugging at top

### `wall-angel-hold` — Wall Angel Hold
- **Set up:** Same wall stack; soft ribs; honest arm contact
- **During:** Breathe into side ribs; scapulae wide/connected, not hard-pinched
- **Pattern:** Isometric scapular position with stack
- **Watch for:** Lumbar arch off wall; breath-hold / neck harden

### `scapular-pushups` — Scapular Push-Ups
- **Set up:** Plank line; straight arms; ribs over hips
- **During:** Protract/retract scapulae only; trunk still
- **Pattern:** Serratus-driven scapular motion on stable trunk
- **Watch for:** Elbow bend into push-up; hip sag/pike

### `band-pull-aparts` — Band Pull-Aparts
- **Set up:** Tall stack; light band tension; scapulae start wide
- **During:** Retract/slightly depress; ribs stay down
- **Pattern:** Mid-back / rear-shoulder control, quiet trunk
- **Watch for:** Upper-trap shrug; lumbar arch at full stretch

### `prone-ytw` — Prone Y-T-W Raises
- **Set up:** Forehead supported; low ribs/pelvis heavy on floor
- **During:** Small honest scapular lifts; no lumbar lift
- **Pattern:** Scapular Y/T/W without lumbar compensation
- **Watch for:** Chest/low-back lift; neck crank

## Core canister / anti-extension & anti-rotation

### `dead-bug` — Dead Bug
- **Set up:** Exhale ribs toward pelvis (ZOA); low back gently heavy
- **During:** Reach only as far as stack holds
- **Pattern:** Anti-extension while limbs move
- **Watch for:** Rib flare overhead; lumbar peel

### `bird-dog` — Bird Dog
- **Set up:** Neutral pelvis; soft brace; square base
- **During:** Level hips; stop before lumbar dip / rib flare
- **Pattern:** Cross-body stability, quiet lumbar
- **Watch for:** Hip twist; arching for height

### `standing-brace-march` — Standing Brace March
- **Set up:** Tall stack; light brace before first step
- **During:** Level pelvis; no lean off stance leg
- **Pattern:** Upright canister under single-leg load
- **Watch for:** Hip hike/drop; rib flare on knee lift

### `wall-supported-carry-march` — Wall Supported Carry March
- **Set up:** Light wall contact (feedback, not lean); stacked before march
- **During:** No trunk rotation; level pelvis
- **Pattern:** Anti-rotation / upright stack with light feedback
- **Watch for:** Shoulder twist; stance-hip collapse

### `band-offset-march-hold` — Band Offset March Hold
- **Set up:** Organize against offset before moving
- **During:** Resist twist; keep level pelvis + brace
- **Pattern:** Anti-rotation under offset load
- **Watch for:** Twist toward anchor; brace loss between steps

## Hinge / pelvis

### `glute-bridges` — Glute Bridges
- **Set up:** Gentle posterior tilt / ribs settle; feet under knees
- **During:** Glute drive; stop before lumbar takeover at top
- **Pattern:** Hip extension with stacked ribs over hips
- **Watch for:** Low-back overextend at top; feet too far (hamstring yank)

### `hip-hinge-drill` — Hip Hinge Drill
- **Set up:** Soft knees; long spine; ribs stacked
- **During:** Hips back; ribs/pelvis travel together
- **Pattern:** Hip-dominant hinge with spinal stack
- **Watch for:** Rounding to reach; squat-pattern knee bend

---

## Ankle (joint-specific)

### `ankle-mobility` — Ankle Mobility Rocks
- **Set up:** Tall square pelvis; heel down; knee over 2nd–3rd toe
- **During:** Honest dorsiflexion; no torso lean/twist cheat
- **Pattern:** Talocrural dorsiflexion with quiet pelvis/trunk
- **Watch for:** Heel lift / arch collapse; torso lean for range

---

## Soft tissue (please scrutinize)

### `foam-roll-upper-back` — Foam Roll Upper Back
- **Set up:** Thoracic placement only; soft ribs (no big dump into extension)
- **During:** Slow roll + breath; protect lumbar
- **Pattern:** Thoracic tissue prep without losing stack
- **Watch for:** Rolling low back; breath-hold into end-range extension
- **Review note:** Centration language is secondary here (tissue prep). Confirm
  you want this framed as stacking coaching vs. keep closer to mobility-only
  cues.

---

## Rotator cuff (please scrutinize machine axis wording)

### `dumbbell-side-lying-external-rotation` — DB Side-Lying ER
- **Set up:** Side-body stack; elbow ~90°; scapula quiet on ribs
- **During:** Smooth arc; no torso roll-back to “finish”
- **Pattern:** ER with stable scapula / quiet trunk
- **Watch for:** Whole-body roll; shrug / elbow drift

### `machine-shoulder-external-rotation` — Machine Shoulder ER
- **Set up:** Match machine pivot to elbow/shoulder axis; tall stack
- **During:** Quiet scapula; no lean to finish
- **Pattern:** Cuff ER with aligned machine axis
- **Watch for:** Too heavy + trunk lean; forcing end range
- **Review note:** Confirm pivot-alignment cue matches how you coach machine ER
  in the gym.
