# Centration cues — review for Sotirios

Warmup, activation, and high-frequency beginner mains now carry structured
joint-centration coaching (`Set up` / `During` / `Pattern` / `Watch for`) in
`packages/engine/src/centrationCues.ts`.

Please scan below **before treating this as final coaching voice**. Prep is
first (already shipped); mains follow in beginner-frequency order, grouped by
pattern. Session UI already uses these for any mapped ID; unmapped exercises
still fall back to legacy `cues[]`.

Source principles used (not invented):

- Ribcage-over-pelvis stacking / canister / zone of apposition
- Neutral pelvis for force transfer
- Scapulae sitting and gliding on the ribcage (protraction, retraction, upward
  rotation as relevant)
- Joint-specific alignment for the drill’s target

---

## Spine mobility

### `cat-cow` — Cat-Cow Flow
- **Set up:** Hands/knees stacked; mid-position ribs/pelvis; long neck
- **During:** Cow/cat from mid-back; pelvis–ribs as one sequence
- **Pattern:** Coordinated spine motion with ribs/pelvis related
- **Watch for:** End-range lumbar hang; shrugging instead of T-spine motion

### `thoracic-rotation` — Thoracic Rotation
- **Set up:** Hips stacked/quiet; exhale to settle ribs
- **During:** Rotate from upper spine; stop when pelvis wants to roll
- **Pattern:** T-spine rotation without lumbar borrow
- **Watch for:** Pelvis rolling open; neck/low-back forcing

---

## Scapular / T-spine (wall + prone + band)

### `wall-slides` — Wall Slides
- **Set up:** Wall contacts + ribs stacked (no lumbar cheat); goal-post arms
- **During:** Scapulae glide on ribs; shorten range before ribs flare
- **Pattern:** Overhead scapular glide with stacked canister
- **Watch for:** Rib flare for wall contact; shrugging at top

### `wall-angel-hold` — Wall Angel Hold
- **Set up:** Same wall stack; soft ribs; honest arm contact
- **During:** Breathe into side ribs; scapulae wide/connected, not hard-pinched
- **Pattern:** Isometric scapular position with stack
- **Watch for:** Lumbar arch off wall; breath-hold / neck harden

### `scapular-pushups` — Scapular Push-Ups
- **Set up:** Plank line; straight arms; ribs over hips
- **During:** Protract/retract scapulae only; trunk still
- **Pattern:** Serratus-driven scapular motion on stable trunk
- **Watch for:** Elbow bend into push-up; hip sag/pike

### `band-pull-aparts` — Band Pull-Aparts
- **Set up:** Tall stack; light band tension; scapulae start wide
- **During:** Retract/slightly depress; ribs stay down
- **Pattern:** Mid-back / rear-shoulder control, quiet trunk
- **Watch for:** Upper-trap shrug; lumbar arch at full stretch

### `prone-ytw` — Prone Y-T-W Raises
- **Set up:** Forehead supported; low ribs/pelvis heavy on floor
- **During:** Small honest scapular lifts; no lumbar lift
- **Pattern:** Scapular Y/T/W without lumbar compensation
- **Watch for:** Chest/low-back lift; neck crank

## Core canister / anti-extension & anti-rotation

### `dead-bug` — Dead Bug
- **Set up:** Exhale ribs toward pelvis (ZOA); low back gently heavy
- **During:** Reach only as far as stack holds
- **Pattern:** Anti-extension while limbs move
- **Watch for:** Rib flare overhead; lumbar peel

### `bird-dog` — Bird Dog
- **Set up:** Neutral pelvis; soft brace; square base
- **During:** Level hips; stop before lumbar dip / rib flare
- **Pattern:** Cross-body stability, quiet lumbar
- **Watch for:** Hip twist; arching for height

### `standing-brace-march` — Standing Brace March
- **Set up:** Tall stack; light brace before first step
- **During:** Level pelvis; no lean off stance leg
- **Pattern:** Upright canister under single-leg load
- **Watch for:** Hip hike/drop; rib flare on knee lift

### `wall-supported-carry-march` — Wall Supported Carry March
- **Set up:** Light wall contact (feedback, not lean); stacked before march
- **During:** No trunk rotation; level pelvis
- **Pattern:** Anti-rotation / upright stack with light feedback
- **Watch for:** Shoulder twist; stance-hip collapse

### `band-offset-march-hold` — Band Offset March Hold
- **Set up:** Organize against offset before moving
- **During:** Resist twist; keep level pelvis + brace
- **Pattern:** Anti-rotation under offset load
- **Watch for:** Twist toward anchor; brace loss between steps

## Hinge / pelvis

### `glute-bridges` — Glute Bridges
- **Set up:** Gentle posterior tilt / ribs settle; feet under knees
- **During:** Glute drive; stop before lumbar takeover at top
- **Pattern:** Hip extension with stacked ribs over hips
- **Watch for:** Low-back overextend at top; feet too far (hamstring yank)

### `hip-hinge-drill` — Hip Hinge Drill
- **Set up:** Soft knees; long spine; ribs stacked
- **During:** Hips back; ribs/pelvis travel together
- **Pattern:** Hip-dominant hinge with spinal stack
- **Watch for:** Rounding to reach; squat-pattern knee bend

---

## Ankle (joint-specific)

### `ankle-mobility` — Ankle Mobility Rocks
- **Set up:** Tall square pelvis; heel down; knee over 2nd–3rd toe
- **During:** Honest dorsiflexion; no torso lean/twist cheat
- **Pattern:** Talocrural dorsiflexion with quiet pelvis/trunk
- **Watch for:** Heel lift / arch collapse; torso lean for range

---

## Soft tissue (please scrutinize)

### `foam-roll-upper-back` — Foam Roll Upper Back
- **Set up:** Thoracic placement only; soft ribs (no big dump into extension)
- **During:** Slow roll + breath; protect lumbar
- **Pattern:** Thoracic tissue prep without losing stack
- **Watch for:** Rolling low back; breath-hold into end-range extension
- **Review note:** Centration language is secondary here (tissue prep). Confirm
  you want this framed as stacking coaching vs. keep closer to mobility-only
  cues.

## Rotator cuff (please scrutinize machine axis wording)

### `dumbbell-side-lying-external-rotation` — DB Side-Lying ER
- **Set up:** Side-body stack; elbow ~90°; scapula quiet on ribs
- **During:** Smooth arc; no torso roll-back to “finish”
- **Pattern:** ER with stable scapula / quiet trunk
- **Watch for:** Whole-body roll; shrug / elbow drift

### `machine-shoulder-external-rotation` — Machine Shoulder ER
- **Set up:** Match machine pivot to elbow/shoulder axis; tall stack
- **During:** Quiet scapula; no lean to finish
- **Pattern:** Cuff ER with aligned machine axis
- **Watch for:** Too heavy + trunk lean; forcing end range
- **Review note:** Confirm pivot-alignment cue matches how you coach machine ER
  in the gym.

## Mains — vertical push (review first; high beginner frequency)

### `dumbbell-shoulder-press` — Dumbbell Shoulder Press
- **Set up:** Ribs stacked; bells at ear height; scapulae on ribcage
- **During:** Press with upward scapular glide; ribs stay down
- **Pattern:** Overhead press with stacked canister
- **Watch for:** Lumbar arch / rib flare; trap shrug / lean-back

### `machine-shoulder-press` — Machine Shoulder Press
- **Set up:** Handles near ear height; pad contact without forced arch
- **During:** Smooth press; quiet head/ribs; no shrug finish
- **Pattern:** Guided OH press with stack
- **Watch for:** Low-back drive into pad; chin jut

## Mains — lateral / rear delts

### `dumbbell-lateral-raise` — Dumbbell Lateral Raise
- **Set up:** Tall stack; soft elbows; scapulae wide (not shrugged)
- **During:** Elbows lead to ~shoulder height; quiet neck
- **Pattern:** Deltoid raise, quiet scapula
- **Watch for:** Trap shrug; swing / lean-back

### `cable-lateral-raise` — Cable Lateral Raise
- **Set up:** Sideways to cable; stacked; scapula set
- **During:** Raise without hike; control eccentric
- **Pattern:** Constant-tension lateral raise
- **Watch for:** Upper-trap hitch; trunk lean

### `cable-rear-delt-fly` — Cable Rear Delt Fly
- **Set up:** Long spine hinge/sit; scapulae start wide
- **During:** Open via scapular glide; stop before lumbar finish
- **Pattern:** Horizontal abduction, quiet trunk
- **Watch for:** Lumbar/head crank; shrug upright-row

### `machine-reverse-pec-deck` — Machine Reverse Pec Deck
- **Set up:** Chest on pad; quiet ribs; soft elbows
- **During:** Open with scapular retract; controlled return
- **Pattern:** Rear shoulder / mid-back on supported trunk
- **Watch for:** Neck/trap yank; bounce out of stretch

## Mains — horizontal push

### `dumbbell-floor-press` — Dumbbell Floor Press
- **Set up:** Soft ribs; scapulae on floor; wrists over elbows
- **During:** Triceps kiss floor; press without rib flare
- **Pattern:** Horizontal press with natural ROM stop
- **Watch for:** Bridging/rib flare; shoulders rolling forward

### `dumbbell-bench-press` — Dumbbell Bench Press
- **Set up:** Feet planted; scapulae set on bench; soft stack
- **During:** Elbows ~30–45°; scapulae stay on bench through lockout
- **Pattern:** Bench press with set scapulae + stack
- **Watch for:** Excess arch; shoulders rolling forward at top

### `machine-chest-press` — Machine Chest Press
- **Set up:** Handles mid-chest; scapulae on pad
- **During:** Even mid-palm press; controlled return
- **Pattern:** Guided horizontal press, scapulae set
- **Watch for:** Shoulders forward at lockout; hard bounce

### `dumbbell-chest-fly` — Dumbbell Chest Fly
- **Set up:** Soft elbows locked; scapulae set; bells over mid-chest
- **During:** Wide arc; honest chest stretch; quiet ribs on return
- **Pattern:** Horizontal adduction, stable scapulae
- **Watch for:** Straight-arm press substitute; too-deep shoulder dump

### `dumbbell-pullover` — Dumbbell Pullover
- **Set up:** Soft ribs on support; slight elbow bend; scapulae set
- **During:** Overhead only as far as ribs stay quiet
- **Pattern:** Long-lever reach with canister control
- **Watch for:** Rib flare for ROM; inconsistent elbow bend
- **Review note:** Confirm preferred bench vs cross-bench setup voice matches
  how you coach this in-session.

## Mains — pull (horizontal + vertical)

### `dumbbell-rows` — Dumbbell Rows
- **Set up:** Long hinged spine; soft knees; scapulae start wide
- **During:** Elbows to hip pocket; quiet torso; protract on lower
- **Pattern:** Hinged horizontal pull, neutral spine
- **Watch for:** Torso rotate; round back / shrug finish

### `single-arm-dumbbell-row` — Single-Arm Dumbbell Row
- **Set up:** Square hips; long spine on support; scapula wide
- **During:** Elbow to hip; no twist-open; controlled protract
- **Pattern:** Unilateral row, quiet trunk
- **Watch for:** Chest open / rotate finish; shoulder hike

### `machine-seated-row` — Machine Seated Row
- **Set up:** Tall sit; arms long; scapulae start wide
- **During:** Soft retract; no lunge-back; controlled return
- **Pattern:** Seated horizontal pull with stack
- **Watch for:** Torso lunge; hard shrug / neck crank

### `cable-seated-row` — Cable Seated Row
- **Set up:** Tall sit; reach without round/over-arch
- **During:** Handle to lower ribs; quiet trunk; long-arm return
- **Pattern:** Cable row with canister stack
- **Watch for:** Rocking momentum; trap finish

### `machine-lat-pulldown` — Machine Lat Pulldown
- **Set up:** Tall sit; thigh pad snug; scapulae ready to depress
- **During:** Scapulae first, then elbows; bar to upper chest
- **Pattern:** Vertical pull with scapular path on stack
- **Watch for:** Arm-only pull; lean-back / lumbar arch

### `cable-lat-pulldown` — Cable Lat Pulldown
- **Set up:** Same tall sit; path in front of face (not behind neck)
- **During:** Depress/down-rotate then pull; control return
- **Pattern:** Cable vertical pull, quiet canister
- **Watch for:** Behind-neck path; torso rock

### `supine-lat-pulldown-isometric` — Supine Lat Pulldown Isometric
- **Set up:** Exhale ribs to pelvis; scapulae wide/connected
- **During:** Tension toward hip pockets; breathe; shoulders down
- **Pattern:** Isometric lat/scap set + anti-extension
- **Watch for:** Rib flare under tension; breath-hold / neck harden

### `seated-lat-sweep-pulse` — Seated Lat Sweep Pulse
- **Set up:** Tall sit; stacked; scapulae on ribs
- **During:** Small lat/scap pulses; ribs stay down
- **Pattern:** Small-range lat control, stacked sit
- **Watch for:** Shrug pulses; stack loss under fatigue
- **Review note:** Confirm pulse size / start position matches your coaching.

### `face-pull` / `cable-face-pull` — Face Pull
- **Set up:** Tall stack; cable ~face height; scapulae wide
- **During:** Pull + ER toward face; ribs down; no lean-back
- **Pattern:** Rear shoulder + ER on quiet trunk
- **Watch for:** Trap upright-row; lumbar extension / momentum

### `prone-swimmer` — Prone Swimmer
- **Set up:** Forehead supported; low ribs/pelvis heavy
- **During:** Honest scapular sweep; lumbar stays down
- **Pattern:** Prone scap / T-spine sequencing
- **Watch for:** Chest/low-back lift; neck crank

### `reverse-snow-angel` — Reverse Snow Angels
- **Set up:** Organized ribs/pelvis; scapulae wide
- **During:** Angel sweep inside mid-back ownership
- **Pattern:** Scapular sweep, anti-compensation
- **Watch for:** Lumbar/head for ROM; shrug at top

## Mains — knee-dominant

### `goblet-squat` — Goblet Squat
- **Set up:** Bell at chest; ribs stacked; soft brace
- **During:** Sit between hips; knees over mid-foot; tall finish
- **Pattern:** Squat with canister stack
- **Watch for:** Chest collapse / rib flare; knee cave / heel lift

### `machine-leg-press` — Machine Leg Press
- **Set up:** Hips/low back on pad; feet where pelvis can stay put
- **During:** Depth only while pelvis stays on pad; mid-foot press
- **Pattern:** Knee-dominant press, organized pelvis
- **Watch for:** Butt wink / pad peel; knee cave

### `split-squat` — Split Squat
- **Set up:** Long split; square hips; stacked torso
- **During:** Soft back knee; front shin quiet; square pelvis
- **Pattern:** Split-stance knee bend, square hips
- **Watch for:** Front knee cave; forward collapse / hip hike

### `dumbbell-reverse-lunge` — Dumbbell Reverse Lunge
- **Set up:** Tall stack; organize over mid-foot before step
- **During:** Square level pelvis; front knee tracks; quiet return
- **Pattern:** Reverse lunge with frontal-plane control
- **Watch for:** Knee/ankle collapse; torso twist under bells

### `dumbbell-bulgarian-split-squat` — Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat
- **Set up:** Front foot far enough for vertical-ish shin; square hips
- **During:** Level pelvis; mid-foot drive; quiet torso
- **Pattern:** RFESS with square pelvis + stack
- **Watch for:** Knee dive; excess lean / lumbar arch to stand

## Mains — hinge / posterior

### `db-rdl` — Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift
- **Set up:** Soft knees; ribs stacked; bells close / lat “pocket”
- **During:** Hips back; long spine; stand by hip extend (not arch)
- **Pattern:** Hip hinge, neutral spine, bells close
- **Watch for:** Round back or squat-pattern; hyperextend finish

### `barbell-hip-thrust` — Barbell Hip Thrust
- **Set up:** Soft ribs; bar over hips; feet for near-vertical shin finish
- **During:** Glute lockout — not lumbar crank; controlled lower
- **Pattern:** Hip extension, posterior chain owns finish
- **Watch for:** Lumbar overextend at top; foot placement that loads low back

### `single-leg-glute-bridge-hold` — Single-Leg Glute Bridge Hold
- **Set up:** Exhale ribs to pelvis; one foot planted
- **During:** Level hips; glute owns hold; quiet ribs
- **Pattern:** SL hip extension hold, level pelvis
- **Watch for:** Lumbar substitute; pelvis twist / hike

## Mains — carries / core

### `suitcase-carry` — Suitcase Carry
- **Set up:** Tall stack before walk; shoulders level
- **During:** Level pelvis; resist side-bend; no loaded shrug
- **Pattern:** Anti-lateral-flexion carry
- **Watch for:** Lean into/away from load; breath-hold shrug

### `farmers-carry` — Farmer's Carry
- **Set up:** Packed shoulders; stacked ribs; quiet neck
- **During:** Smooth gait; no forward lean / rock
- **Pattern:** Loaded gait, packed shoulders + stack
- **Watch for:** Forward lean / arch under fatigue; shrug + apnea

### `plank` — Plank
- **Set up:** Line; ribs toward pelvis; scapulae wide on ribs
- **During:** No sag/pike; breathe; push floor away
- **Pattern:** Anti-extension hold with stack
- **Watch for:** Hip sag / rib flare; breath-hold / shoulder hike

### `hollow-body-hold` — Hollow Body Hold
- **Set up:** Exhale ZOA; levers only as long as stack allows
- **During:** Hold hollow; shorten levers if lumbar peels
- **Pattern:** Anti-extension hollow with ZOA
- **Watch for:** Lumbar peel for bigger shape; neck crank

## Mains — arms / calves (joint stacking; quiet trunk)

### `overhead-cable-triceps-extension` — Overhead Cable Triceps Extension
- **Set up:** Stack before arms overhead; quiet upper arms; no shrug start
- **During:** Elbow motion only; ribs stay down
- **Pattern:** OH elbow extension without lumbar borrow
- **Watch for:** Rib flare / lean-back; elbows flare off path

### `cable-biceps-curl` — Cable Biceps Curl
- **Set up:** Tall stack; elbows near sides; shoulders down
- **During:** No swing; controlled lower; quiet scapulae
- **Pattern:** Elbow flexion, quiet trunk
- **Watch for:** Hip thrust momentum; shoulders rolling forward

### `hammer-curl` — Hammer Curl
- **Set up:** Neutral grip; stacked; elbows at sides
- **During:** Thumbs-up curl; no low-back swing
- **Pattern:** Neutral-grip elbow flexion
- **Watch for:** Heaving reps; shrug at top

### `db-calf-raise` — Dumbbell Calf Raise
- **Set up:** Tall stack; mid-foot pressure; soft-straight knees
- **During:** Rise on clean foot line; controlled lower
- **Pattern:** Plantarflexion with stacked posture
- **Watch for:** Outer-foot roll / arch collapse; bounce + lean
